## 2. Getting reviews

Ask in person right after a happy purchase — that works far better than asking later.

**WhatsApp message to send after a sale** (get your review link from Google
Business Profile → "Ask for reviews"):

English:
> Thank you for shopping at SSS Bags! 😊 If you liked your bag, could you leave us
> a quick Google review? It really helps a small local shop.
> [REVIEW LINK]

Hindi / Hinglish:
> SSS Bags se shopping karne ke liye dhanyavaad! 😊 Agar aapko bag pasand aaya ho
> to please Google par ek chhota sa review de dijiye — isse humari bahut madad hogi.
> [REVIEW LINK]

Punjabi:
> SSS Bags ਤੋਂ ਖਰੀਦਦਾਰੀ ਕਰਨ ਲਈ ਧੰਨਵਾਦ! 😊 ਜੇ ਤੁਹਾਨੂੰ ਬੈਗ ਪਸੰਦ ਆਇਆ ਤਾਂ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਰਕੇ
> Google ਤੇ ਇੱਕ ਛੋਟਾ ਜਿਹਾ ਰਿਵਿਊ ਦੇ ਦਿਓ — ਇਸ ਨਾਲ ਸਾਡੀ ਬਹੁਤ ਮਦਦ ਹੋਵੇਗੀ।
> [REVIEW LINK]

**Replying to reviews** (reply to every one, within a day or two):
- 5★: "Thank you, [name]! So glad you liked the [bag]. See you again soon at SSS Bags 😊"
- Low rating: "Sorry to hear this, [name]. Please call us on 70091 83833 so we can make it right."

Don't offer discounts in exchange for reviews — Google's rules forbid it and can remove them.
